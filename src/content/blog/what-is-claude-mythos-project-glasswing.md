Claude Mythos is the name of Anthropic's most capable model tier, and Project Glasswing is the program that first put it in the hands of a small group of security partners. Because Mythos is not available to the general public, many people are unsure what it is, how it differs from Claude Fable, and how anyone gets access. This guide explains all of it in plain English.

## Quick Answer

- **Mythos** is a model tier that sits above Opus in Anthropic's lineup.
- **Project Glasswing** is Anthropic's initiative to use the Mythos-class models to help secure critical software, with access limited to selected organizations.
- **Fable** is the general-release version of the same underlying model, with extra safeguards for biology and cybersecurity.
- The current Mythos-tier models are **Claude Mythos 5.2** and **Claude Fable 5.2**, released on November 9, 2026.

---

## Where Mythos Started: Project Glasswing

On April 7, 2026, Anthropic announced Project Glasswing alongside a model called **Claude Mythos Preview**. Anthropic described Glasswing as an initiative to secure the world's most critical software by giving selected organizations early access to a frontier model.

Mythos Preview is a general-purpose model, and Anthropic called it its most capable yet for coding and agentic tasks. The company said the model's coding ability was strong enough to find and exploit software vulnerabilities at a level that only the most skilled humans could match. That is why Anthropic chose to share it with defenders first rather than release it widely.

### Key details of the launch:
- Access went to a limited set of partner organizations that maintain important software infrastructure.
- Anthropic committed $100 million in model usage credits to cover Glasswing participants during the research preview.
- Participants could use the model through the Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud's Vertex AI and Microsoft Foundry, at a published price of $25 per million input tokens and $125 per million output tokens.
- Anthropic said it planned to bring Mythos-class models to users eventually, but only once safeguards were ready.

---

## From Mythos Preview to Fable and Mythos 5

On June 9, 2026, Anthropic released two models built on the same underlying system: **Claude Fable 5** for general use, and **Claude Mythos 5** for restricted access through Glasswing. Fable carries additional safeguards aimed at cybersecurity and biology, which is what allowed Anthropic to offer it widely.

Shortly after launch, both models were switched off for several weeks because of US export controls.

## Mythos vs. Fable vs. Opus

| Attribute | Mythos 5.2 | Fable 5.2 | Opus 5.5 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier** | Mythos | Mythos | Opus |
| **Who can use it** | Vetted organizations | General users | General users |
| **Safeguards** | Lifted in some areas | Extra biology and cyber safeguards | Safeguards similar to Fable 5.1 |
| **Released** | Nov 9, 2026 | Nov 9, 2026 | Sep 22, 2026 |

Mythos 5.2 and Fable 5.2 share the same underlying model. The difference is mainly the safeguards and who is allowed to use it. Anthropic also noted that Opus 5.5 is comparable to Mythos 5.1 in biology and cybersecurity, so it deploys Opus 5.5 with safeguards similar to those on Fable 5.1.

## Why Isn't Mythos Public?

The main reason is **cybersecurity**. A model that is very good at finding software weaknesses can help defenders fix them, but it could also help attackers. Anthropic's approach is to release the most capable version gradually, giving vetted defenders access first and shipping a safeguarded version, Fable, to everyone else.

---

## How Can You Get Access?

Mythos access is not a self-serve sign-up. It is tied to vetted programs such as Project Glasswing, which focuses on organizations responsible for important software and security work. If you represent such an organization, the best place to start is Anthropic's official Glasswing page. Separately, vetted organizations can apply to a Life Sciences Verification Program to use Opus 5.5 for biology research.

If you are an individual user or a small team, **Fable 5.2** is the closest option you can use today.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Claude Mythos?
Claude Mythos is Anthropic's top model tier, sitting above Opus. Its models are restricted to vetted organizations because of their strong cybersecurity capabilities.

### What is Project Glasswing?
Project Glasswing is Anthropic's initiative, announced on April 7, 2026, to use Mythos-class models to help secure critical software, with early access limited to selected partners.

### What is the difference between Claude Mythos and Claude Fable?
They share the same underlying model. Fable has extra safety measures for biology and cybersecurity and is available to general users. Mythos has those safeguards lifted in some areas and is restricted.

### Can I use Claude Mythos for free?
No. Mythos is not publicly available, and Glasswing participants have used credits that Anthropic funded for the research preview.

### What are the newest Mythos-tier models?
Claude Mythos 5.2 and Claude Fable 5.2, released on November 9, 2026.
